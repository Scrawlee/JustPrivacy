## What it is

Your browser reveals details such as screen size, fonts, language and graphics hardware. Taken together, they can form an identifier that is often unique.

## Why it is hard to block

These details are needed for pages to display properly, so they cannot simply be switched off. Blocking them can even make your browser stand out more.

## Reducing exposure

- Use a browser that limits the information it exposes.
- Avoid unusual customizations and rarely used extensions.
- Keep your browser up to date.
